MATLAB TBM CHPC Pipeline

Active Development

Batch Transport-Based Morphometry on SLURM clusters. Registers subject Fractional Anisotropy maps to the HCP1065 FA template using multi-scale Volumetric Optimal Transport, computes deformation fields identifying regions of white matter expansion and contraction, and exports Jacobian determinant NIfTI maps for downstream ML/statistical analysis. Includes batch gallery PNG generation for QC.

MATLABSLURMHPCNIfTIVOTMEX

MaPPeRTrac CHPC Pipeline

Active Development

Lab-specific version of MaPPeRTrac adapted for WashU CHPC. Automates DICOM-to-BIDS conversion, batch DWI preprocessing (denoising, Gibbs correction, eddy current correction), FreeSurfer recon-all for cortical/subcortical segmentation, bedpostX fiber orientation modeling, probabilistic tractography via probtrackX, and bidirectionally validated connectome matrix generation.
